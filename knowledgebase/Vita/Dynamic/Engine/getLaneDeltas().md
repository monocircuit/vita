There are three cases:

### Both [[LinearChronicle|LinearChronicles]] are finite
Should both [[LinearChronicle|LinearChronicles]] be finite, then both left and right deltas are defined.

### [[LinearChronicle|LinearChronicleA]] is not finite
Then the right delta will be `Infinity`.

### [[LinearChronicle|LinearChronicleB]] is not finite
Then the left delta will be `Infinity`.
