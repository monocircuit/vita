### What is a [[Vita]]?
A [[Vita]] is basically just a fancy name for a classic CV, but we at Monocircuit offer different types of these CVs

### Why call it [[Vita]]?
[[Vita]] is the academic short hand for Cirriculum Vitae, so its an accurate name for what we are trying to describe with it. Also the name [[Vita]] is way more beautiful to represent in code or as a company product.

### What Plural to Use for the Word [[Vita]]?
The probably correct wording would be [[Vita|Vitae]], since [[Vita]] is a latin word and this would be the correct latin plural for it... But [[Vita|Vitae]] is quite clumsy and unintuitive, this [[Vita|Vitas]] would be a nicer plural to use.
