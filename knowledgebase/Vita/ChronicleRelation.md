### When do [[ChronicleRelation|ChronicleRelations]] make sense?
Such relations make sense, if, for example, two work experiences are related: If my dad (who works for tegut makes a course in something, this course should be related to the orginal work place that gave him this opportunity)

### Display [[ChronicleRelation|ChronicleRelations]] accordingly
In order to display [[ChronicleRelation|ChronicleRelations]] in a proper matter on the [[Vita]] they need to be grouped. Meaning that they preserve proximity.
